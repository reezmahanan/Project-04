7-Day Easy Returns & Exchanges

We guarantee your complete satisfaction with every piece.

Step 1

Request Exchange

WhatsApp our concierge at +94 77 123 4567 or email support within 7 days of delivery with your order ID.

Step 2

Doorstep Courier Pickup

Our courier partner will visit your address anywhere in Sri Lanka to collect the return parcel.

Step 3

Replacement Dispatched

Your replacement size or store credit voucher is processed immediately upon inspection.

Simple Exchange Guidelines:

  • • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have original designer tags attached.
  • • Footwear must be tested indoors on clean surfaces with zero scuffing.
  • • Handloom and silk pieces must be returned in their protective dust bag.
